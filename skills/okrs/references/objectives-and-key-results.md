# Objectives and key results

OKRs are a collaborative goal-setting method for a company, team, or individual. Andy Grove developed them at Intel from Peter Drucker's management by objectives, and John Doerr brought them to Google in 1999.

## Objective

The **what**. An objective is significant, concrete, action-oriented, and ideally inspirational. It is a direction worth pursuing, not a number.

- Well-designed objectives are an antidote to fuzzy thinking and fuzzy execution.
- An objective can be long-lived; its key results change as progress is made.

## Key results

The **how**. Key results benchmark and monitor how you reach the objective. They are specific, time-bound, aggressive yet realistic, and above all measurable and verifiable.

- "If it doesn't have a number, it's not a key result" (Marissa Mayer, quoted by Doerr).
- At the end of the cycle there is no argument: a key result was met or it wasn't.
- Each objective has three to five key results. Achieving all of them should mean the objective is achieved.
- Key results describe outcomes, not activities. Tasks belong in a plan or to-do list.

## Formula

"I **will** (objective) **as measured by** (key results)."

## Example

| | |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Make onboarding the reason new customers stay. |
| **KR 1** | 60% of new accounts complete a first project within 7 days (from 35%). |
| **KR 2** | Median time to first value under 10 minutes (from 25). |
| **KR 3** | Onboarding support tickets per new account below 0.2 (from 0.5). |

## Pairing key results

Grove recommended pairing key results so that hitting one does not damage another. Pair a quantity measure with a quality measure:

| Quantity | Paired quality |
|---|---|
| Ship 10 new features. | Fewer than 3 regressions reported per release. |
| Close 50 new deals. | 90-day churn of new accounts below 5%. |
| Answer tickets within 2 hours. | Customer satisfaction on tickets above 4.5/5. |

## Objective vs. key result vs. task

| Objective | Key result | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Win the mid-market segment. | 40 new mid-market customers this quarter. | Hire two account executives. |
| Make the product fast. | p95 page load under 1 second. | Add a CDN. |

If an item is something you *do* rather than something you *achieve*, it is a task.
