# Strategy Kernel

These concepts help you craft and judge strategy. They are drawn from Richard Rumelt's *Good Strategy / Bad Strategy* and *The Crux*. A strategy is a coherent response to an important challenge, and its minimum structure, the **kernel**, has three parts: a **diagnosis** of the critical challenge, a **guiding policy** for overcoming it, and **coherent actions** that carry out the policy. The parts depend on each other: a weak diagnosis produces a vague policy, and a vague policy produces a wish list of actions.
