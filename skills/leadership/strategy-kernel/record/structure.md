# Strategy record

A durable record of the current strategy.

```
strategy-kernel/
├── README.md               # the kernel on one page, linking to the parts
├── diagnosis.md
├── guiding-policy.md
└── coherent-actions.md
```

Templates are a starting point: add, drop, or rename sections to fit what you learn.

- [Diagnosis](templates/diagnosis.md)
- [Guiding policy](templates/guiding-policy.md)
- [Coherent actions](templates/coherent-actions.md)

After creating a record, offer to add it to the [Strategic Context](templates/strategic-context.md).
