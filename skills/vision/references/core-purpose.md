# Core purpose

The core purpose is the organization's fundamental reason for being, beyond just making money. Together with [core values](core-values.md), it forms the **core ideology**.

## Principles

- **A guiding star, never reached.** A purpose is never fully achieved, unlike a goal or strategy. It guides and inspires indefinitely.
- **Not a product or market.** It describes why the organization exists, not what it currently sells or to whom.
- **Short and broad.** Usually one sentence, broad enough to last a century.
- **Authentic.** It should capture the idealistic motivations of the people doing the work, not an external audience.

## Examples (cited by Collins and Porras)

| Organization | Core purpose |
|---|---|
| 3M | To solve unsolved problems innovatively. |
| Merck | To preserve and improve human life. |
| Walt Disney | To make people happy. |
| Walmart | To give ordinary folk the chance to buy the same things as rich people. |

## Finding it: the five whys

Start with a descriptive statement such as "We make X products" or "We deliver X services". Then ask "Why is that important?" five times. After a few whys, you reach the fundamental purpose.

Example: "We build accounting software." Why? "So small businesses get their books right." Why? "So owners stop losing time and money on admin." Why? "So they can focus on the work they started the business to do." That last answer is closer to a purpose.

## Tests for a core purpose

- Could it guide the organization for 100 years?
- Would it still hold if the current products and markets disappeared?
- Does it inspire the people inside, not only sound good to outsiders?
- Can it never be fully finished?

## Failure modes

- **Product description.** "We make the best CRM for SMBs" is a business definition, not a purpose.
- **Goal disguised as purpose.** "Become the market leader" can be achieved, so it's a goal.
- **Profit as purpose.** Making money is a necessity, not a reason for being.
- **Marketing copy.** Written to impress customers, not to guide the people doing the work.
