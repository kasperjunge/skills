# Vision

These concepts help you craft and judge a vision. They are drawn from Jim Collins and Jerry Porras's *Built to Last* and their Harvard Business Review article "Building Your Company's Vision". A vision has two parts. The **core ideology** defines what the organization stands for and why it exists; it is discovered, not invented, and should stay the same for decades. The **envisioned future** defines what it aspires to become; it is bold, specific, and meant to change once reached. The guiding principle is **preserve the core, stimulate progress**: hold the ideology fixed and be willing to change everything else, including strategies, practices, and products.

A vision is not a strategy. It sets the long-term direction; a strategy tackles the critical challenge now. See the `strategy-kernel` skill, and the `okrs` skill for near-term goals.
