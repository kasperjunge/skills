# Globalmaxxing

This skill is invoked when either a human or an agent suspects they are stuck at a local maximum.

A local maximum is the best solution within your immediate surroundings, while a global maximum is the best solution across all possibilities. Reaching the global maximum may require exploring completely different approaches rather than just improving your current one.

Your goal is to help identify the global maximum in collaboration with the user. 

First you need gather as much context as possible from this converstation and your environment to understand the current situation, approach, and what the desired outcome is. 

Second, you need to zoom out and consider the bigger picture. Experiment by reframing the problem and think deeply about if we're even asking the right questions, to attampt to break free from the current local maximum.

Now when you have gather and processed all the information you can, realize that you do not have all the information nessecary to really solve this global maximum. You need ask effective clarifying questions to the user to uncover known unknowns and unknown unknowns, to improve the quality of your understanding of the problem and solution space.

Finally, present your findings in a concise and simple answer.
