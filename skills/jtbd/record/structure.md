# Jobs record

A durable record of jobs and the interviews behind them.

```
jtbd/
├── README.md                    # job map: jobs and how they relate
├── jobs/
│   └── <job>/
│       ├── README.md            # the job
│       ├── competition.md       # optional
│       ├── forces.md            # optional
│       ├── timeline.md          # optional
│       └── sub-jobs/
│           └── <job>/           # same layout, any depth
└── interviews/
    └── <yyyy-mm-dd>-<who>.md    # raw transcript or notes, as captured
```

Jobs cite the interviews they draw on.

Templates are a starting point: add, drop, or rename sections to fit what you learn.

- [Job](templates/job.md)
- [Forces](templates/forces.md)
- [Timeline](templates/timeline.md)

After creating a record, offer to add it to the [Strategic Context](templates/strategic-context.md).
