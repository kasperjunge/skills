# Jobs To Be Done

These concepts explain why people buy and switch products. They are drawn from Clayton Christensen's *Competing Against Luck* and Bob Moesta's *Demand-Side Sales 101*. The core idea: people don't buy products, they **hire** them to make **progress** in a particular **circumstance**. Understand the job and you understand what the product must do, what it competes with, and what stops people from switching. The concepts build on each other: the job defines the competition, and the forces and timeline explain how people move from one solution to another.
