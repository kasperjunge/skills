# Positioning record

A durable record of the positioning and the evidence behind it.

```
positioning/
├── README.md                   # the positioning canvas for the product
├── evidence/
│   └── <yyyy-mm-dd>-<source>.md  # interviews, win/loss notes, sales calls
└── pitch.md                    # optional: the sales narrative built on it
```

For several products or distinct segments, use one directory per product under `positioning/`, each with its own `README.md`.

Components cite the evidence they draw on. Mark components without evidence as assumptions.

Templates are a starting point: add, drop, or rename sections to fit what you learn.

- [Positioning canvas](templates/canvas.md)
- [Sales pitch](templates/pitch.md)

After creating a record, offer to add it to the [Strategic Context](templates/strategic-context.md).
